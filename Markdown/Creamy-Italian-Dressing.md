# Creamy Italian Dressing

*A quick whisked dressing with mayonnaise, olive oil, and Parmesan carrying the classic Italian herb blend.*

**Servings:** Makes 2 cups | **Prep Time:** ~5 min | **Cook Time:** None | **Total Time:** ~5 min

## About This Recipe

A no-cook dressing that comes together with a single whisking step — good for topping salads or using as a dip.

<!-- Add photo: ./Creamy-Italian-Dressing.jpg -->

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/3 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
- 3 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon dried basil
- 1/2 teaspoon dried parsley flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper

## Directions

1. In a small bowl, whisk all ingredients together until fully blended.

## Notes & Tips

- Per 2-tablespoon serving: 92 calories, 10g fat (2g saturated), 3mg cholesterol, 126mg sodium, 1g carbohydrate (1g sugar), 0g protein.
