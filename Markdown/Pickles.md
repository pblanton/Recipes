# Sour or Half Sour Pickles

*Naturally fermented deli-style pickles, made with a simple salt brine and no vinegar.*

**Servings:** Makes 1 quart | **Prep Time:** ~40 min active | **Cook Time:** None (ferments 5 days)

## About This Recipe

A true fermentation pickle rather than a vinegar quick-pickle — just cucumbers, a salt brine, and time. Salt concentration determines whether you end up with a half sour or a fully sour pickle.

## Ingredients

- Freshly picked, firm, unwaxed, bumpy pickling cucumbers (Kirby cucumbers)
- 2 cloves spring garlic, thinly sliced (optional)
- 1 dill flower, or 5 sprigs fresh dill, or 1 teaspoon dill seed (optional)
- 1/2 teaspoon coriander seed (optional)
- 1/2 jalapeño, seeded and slivered (optional)
- 2 tablespoons kosher (non-iodized) salt for half sour, or 3 tablespoons for sour, per quart of water

## Directions

**Brine**

1. Dissolve the appropriate amount of kosher salt in a quart of cold water, stirring until fully dissolved. Use distilled water if you're on city (chlorinated) water; well or spring water is fine as-is.

**Packing**

1. Soak the cucumbers in ice water for 30 minutes.
2. Trim the blossom end of each cucumber (opposite the stem) — this lets the brine wick into the cucumber more easily.
3. For extra crunch, add a bay leaf, grape leaf, or black tea leaves to the jar.
4. Pack the cucumbers and any optional aromatics into a clean quart jar, leaving enough headspace for a fermentation weight.
5. Fill the jar with brine, making sure everything is fully submerged. Drop in the fermentation weight.
6. Seal with a fermentation airlock lid and set the jar on the counter for 5 days. Leave it alone — no need to check or fuss with it during fermentation.

## Notes & Tips

- **Kahm yeast** (a stringy white film) isn't harmful but should be skimmed off if it appears.
- **Colored or fuzzy mold** means the batch should be discarded.
- **Cloudy brine and sediment** are normal and not a sign of spoilage.
- Starting with truly clean equipment is the best defense against mold: wash the jars well, then bake them upside down at 350°F for about 30 minutes. Let them cool in the closed oven, then use them right away, straight from the oven.
- This recipe references specific equipment by product name — a silicone fermentation airlock lid (sold as "Pickle Pipe") and a glass fermentation weight (sold as "Pickle Puck") — which are what keep the cucumbers submerged and let fermentation gases escape without letting air or contaminants in.
