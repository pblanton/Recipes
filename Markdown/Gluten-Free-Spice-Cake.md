# Gluten-Free Spice Cake

*Gluten-free option. A warmly spiced layer cake or cupcake batter — ginger, cinnamon, nutmeg, allspice, and clove — built on an all-purpose gluten-free flour blend.*

**Servings:** One 2-layer 8-inch cake, or about 28 cupcakes | **Prep Time:** 25 min | **Cook Time:** 30 min | **Total Time:** ~1 hour (cake only, unfrosted)

## About This Recipe

A straightforward creamed-butter spice cake, gluten-free flour standing in for wheat with powdered milk added back in for richness and structure. Works equally well as a two-layer cake or a big batch of cupcakes.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups (405 g) all-purpose gluten-free flour, plus more for dusting pans
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1/4 cup powdered milk
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons ground ginger
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/2 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves
- 1/2 cup butter
- 2 cups brown sugar or coconut palm sugar
- 4 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
- 1 cup milk or non-dairy alternative

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (static) or 325°F (convection). Bring the eggs and milk to room temperature.
2. Spray two 8-inch round cake pans with non-stick spray and dust lightly with gluten-free flour. For cupcakes, oil or line cupcake pans (makes about 28 cupcakes).
3. Whisk together the flour, powdered milk, baking powder, salt, and spices; set aside.
4. In a large mixing bowl, beat the butter and sugar with the paddle attachment until very light and fluffy, about 3–4 minutes.
5. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Mix in the vanilla with the last egg.
6. Alternate adding the milk and the flour mixture, beating briefly between each addition, until smooth.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared pans and let it rest on the counter for 10 minutes before baking.
8. Bake cakes for 30 minutes (turning the pans halfway through if using convection); check cupcakes for doneness starting at 20 minutes. A cake tester or toothpick inserted in the center should come out clean or with just a few dry crumbs, and the cake should be pulling away slightly from the pan sides. Add time as needed.
9. When done, turn off the oven and leave the door open, letting the cakes cool in the oven for about 5 minutes. After 15–20 minutes total cooling time, gently invert the cakes out of the pans onto a cooling rack, then flip them back over to finish cooling right-side up.
