# Baking Powder Biscuits

*A basic drop-or-cut biscuit dough that works equally well as tidy rounds or quick drop biscuits.*

**Servings:** Not specified in original | **Prep Time:** ~10 min | **Cook Time:** 15–20 min | **Total Time:** ~30 min

## About This Recipe

A flexible, no-fuss biscuit dough — the same base works as rustic drop biscuits or rolled-and-cut rounds, depending on how much time you want to spend on shaping.

<!-- Add photo: ./Baking-Powder-Biscuits.jpg -->

## Ingredients

- 3 cups biscuit flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1 to 4 tablespoons sugar, to taste
- 4 to 6 tablespoons butter or beef tallow
- 1 cup milk, buttermilk, or water

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
2. Mix the dry ingredients together. Using two knives, a pastry blender, or your fingertips, cut or rub in the cold butter (or tallow) until the mixture resembles coarse breadcrumbs.
3. Add the liquid all at once and mix quickly and gently, about 20 seconds, until a soft dough forms.
4. **For drop biscuits:** Drop the dough by the spoonful onto a lightly floured baking sheet, or fill the cups of a greased muffin tin about two-thirds full.
5. **For cut biscuits:** Pat the dough into a rectangle about 3/4 inch thick. Fold it into thirds like a letter, then roll gently with a floured rolling pin back out to 3/4 inch thick.
6. Cut into rounds with a biscuit cutter, or cut into squares or diamonds with a bench knife or bowl scraper to avoid leftover scraps.
7. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes, until lightly browned. Remove from the oven and serve warm.

## Notes & Tips

- Beef tallow is the preferred fat for this binder — refined/deodorized tallow gives the closest flavor and texture to lard while keeping things clean. Keep it cold going into the flour, same as butter.
- Folding the dough into thirds before the final roll (the "letter fold") builds flakier layers than rolling it out just once.
