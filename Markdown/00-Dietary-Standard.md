# Dietary Standard for This Binder

Every recipe in this binder follows the standards below. Individual recipes don't repeat this information — if a recipe here differs from its original source, it's because of these rules.

## Biblically Clean

All recipes are adapted to include only foods considered clean per Leviticus 11:

- **Land animals:** must both chew the cud and have a split hoof (beef, lamb, goat are clean; pork is not and is excluded from every recipe).
- **Water creatures:** must have both fins and scales (fish like tilapia, cod, salmon, and halibut are clean; shellfish and mollusks — shrimp, crab, scallops, squid, lobster, clams, etc. — are not and are excluded).
- **Birds:** common poultry (chicken, turkey, duck, quail) is clean; birds of prey and scavengers are not.
- **Organ meats** from a clean animal (e.g., beef tripe, chicken gizzards) are treated as clean.
- Insects, and any water creature without both fins and scales, are excluded entirely.

Where a source recipe called for an unclean ingredient, it's been removed or swapped for a clean equivalent that keeps the dish's flavor and texture intent.

## Fats & Oils

- Any recipe originally calling for rapeseed (canola) oil or peanut oil uses **avocado oil** instead, across the board.
- Where a recipe calls for lard or vegetable shortening, **beef tallow** is the preferred fat — it behaves the same as lard in dough (similar melting point, cuts into flour the same way) while avoiding both the pork-derived source and the seed-oil-based shortenings. Refined/deodorized tallow is the closest in flavor to lard; keep it cold, same as butter.
