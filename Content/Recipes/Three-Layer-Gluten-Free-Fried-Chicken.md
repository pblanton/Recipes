# Three-Layer Gluten-Free Fried Chicken

*Gluten-free option. A three-stage dry-wet-dry coating (potato starch, buttermilk slurry, seasoned gum-free flour) for extra-crunchy fried chicken.*

**Servings:** 1 whole chicken, cut up | **Prep Time:** ~30 min plus 30 min rest | **Cook Time:** ~10 min per batch | **Total Time:** ~1.5 hours

## About This Recipe

The three-layer approach — dry starch, then a bubbly buttermilk-and-leavening dip, then a seasoned gum-free gluten-free flour — builds a thicker, crunchier crust than a single dredge. Quantities below are for one chicken; scale up as needed.

## Ingredients

**First Layer: Dry Coat**
- 1/2 cup potato starch

**Second Layer: Wet Dip**
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 cup buttermilk (or 3/4 cup whole milk plus 1/4 cup yogurt)

**Third Layer: Breading & Spices**
- 2 cups gum-free gluten-free flour mix (recipe below)
- 1/4 cup potato flakes
- 1 spice mix, your choice (options below)

**Gum-Free Gluten-Free Flour Mix** (combine in these ratios)
- 1 part tapioca starch flour
- 2 parts potato starch
- 3 parts superfine white rice flour

**Frying**
- Oil for frying, about 2 inches deep in the pan (avocado oil recommended — it's the binder's preferred oil and holds up well at frying temperatures)

## Spice Mix Options

Pick one of the following for the Third Layer:

**Philip's Spice Mix** (uses Sue Spice in place of cayenne)
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 1/2 teaspoons thyme
- 1 1/2 teaspoons basil
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon Sue Spice (see Notes)
- 1 tablespoon black pepper
- 4 teaspoons paprika
- 2 tablespoons white pepper

**Classic 11 Herbs & Spices Mix** (scaled to season 2 cups flour, matching the Third Layer above)
- 2/3 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon basil
- 1/3 teaspoon oregano
- 1 teaspoon celery salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon dried mustard
- 4 teaspoons paprika
- 2 teaspoons garlic salt
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 3 teaspoons white pepper

## Directions

1. Work with half the chicken at a time. Shake the pieces in a bag with the potato starch to coat, then set aside.
2. Beat the egg, baking powder, and baking soda together in a medium bowl; stir in the buttermilk (it will bubble and foam).
3. Whisk the flour mix, potato flakes, and your chosen spice mix together in a shallow dish.
4. One at a time, dip the starch-coated chicken pieces in the buttermilk mixture, shake off the excess, then dredge through the seasoned flour mix until well coated. Optionally, repeat the wet-then-dry dip a second time for a thicker crust — if you do, increase all Second and Third Layer measurements by 25% to have enough coating.
5. Set the coated chicken on a wire rack, cover with plastic wrap, and let rest for 30 minutes.
6. Preheat the oven to 200°F.
7. Add about 2 inches of oil to a large skillet or high-sided Dutch oven (the high sides help contain splatter) and heat to about 350°F.
8. Place the chicken skin-side down and fry for about 5 minutes, then turn and fry about 5 minutes more. Watch for hot spots and adjust position as needed.
9. Remove pieces once they reach an internal temperature of 160°F (breasts) or 175°F (thighs and legs). Transfer to a paper-towel-lined, oven-safe platter and hold in the 200°F oven until ready to serve.

## Notes & Tips

- **Sue Spice** is a homemade dried hot pepper powder — jalapeños from a farm in Pueblo, sliced on a slicer, sun-dried on custom-built racks, then ground into powder at a local Asian grocery. Very spicy, used almost exclusively in place of cayenne. Named after a Vietnamese woman named Sue.
- Frying oil type wasn't specified in the original; avocado oil fits the binder's standard and has a smoke point well above the 350°F frying temperature here.
