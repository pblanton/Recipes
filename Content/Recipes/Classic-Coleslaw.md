# Classic Coleslaw

*A simple mayo-based coleslaw with both green and red cabbage, carrots, and Vidalia onion.*

**Servings:** Not specified in original | **Prep Time:** ~15 min, plus 30 min chill | **Cook Time:** None | **Total Time:** ~45 min

## About This Recipe

A straightforward three-step slaw: whisk the dressing, shred the vegetables, combine and chill. The mix of green and red cabbage keeps it colorful.

![Classic Coleslaw](./Classic-Coleslaw.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 6 tablespoons sugar
- 1/4 cup cider vinegar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 head green cabbage
- 1/2 head red cabbage
- 2 large carrots (about 5 oz.)
- 1 small Vidalia onion (about 6 oz.)

## Directions

1. Whisk together the mayonnaise, sugar, vinegar, salt, and pepper in a large bowl. Refrigerate until ready to use.
2. Shred the cabbages, carrots, and onion on the large holes of a box grater, or with a food processor.
3. Stir the shredded vegetables into the dressing. Chill for at least 30 minutes to let the flavors marry, then serve.

## Notes & Tips

- The directions in the source recipe mention shredding a jalapeño along with the cabbage, carrots, and onion, but no jalapeño appears in the ingredient list or amount. Left out here since there's no quantity to go on — add one, minced or shredded, if you want the extra kick.
