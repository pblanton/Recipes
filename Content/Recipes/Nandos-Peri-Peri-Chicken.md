# Nando's Peri-Peri Chicken

*A copycat of Nando's peri-peri hot sauce, used to marinate and roast or grill a whole spatchcocked chicken.*

**Servings:** 1 whole chicken (sauce yields ~300 ml, enough for one chicken with extra for serving) | **Prep Time:** ~20 min, plus overnight marinating | **Cook Time:** ~50 min roasted, or grilled

## About This Recipe

A two-part recipe: a simmered-then-blended chile sauce first, and a simple overnight-marinated spatchcocked chicken second. Scale the sauce up freely if you want extra for basting or serving on the side.

## Ingredients

**Peri-Peri Sauce** (yields about 300 ml)
- 3 bird's eye chiles, coarsely chopped (up to 8 for very hot)
- 3 red jalapeño peppers, coarsely chopped
- 1 red bell pepper (capsicum), seeded and coarsely chopped
- 5 cloves garlic, peeled and coarsely chopped
- 2 tablespoons oregano
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 70 ml white vinegar
- 2 teaspoons salt

**Peri-Peri Chicken**
- 1 whole chicken, spatchcocked
- 2 teaspoons salt
- Peri-peri sauce (from above)

## Directions

**Sauce**

1. Pour the olive oil and vinegar into a saucepan over medium heat. Once it begins to simmer, add the bell pepper, garlic, and lemon juice. Simmer for 15 minutes.
2. Turn off the heat and add the jalapeños (with seeds), bird's eye chiles, oregano, and salt. Stir and let it sit until cool enough to blend.
3. Blend until smooth, creamy, and bright orange.
4. Keeps refrigerated up to one month, or freezes indefinitely.

**Chicken**

1. One day before cooking, coat the spatchcocked chicken with the peri-peri sauce and seal in a freezer bag. Marinate overnight in the refrigerator.
2. Either roast in the oven at 400°F for 30 minutes, then reduce to 325°F for another 20 minutes; or grill slowly over indirect heat.

## Notes & Tips

- Bird's eye chiles are the main heat control — scale from 3 up to 8 depending on how hot you want it.
- The sauce recipe is easy to double or triple if you want extra for basting during cooking or serving alongside.
