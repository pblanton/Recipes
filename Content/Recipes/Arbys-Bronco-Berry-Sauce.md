# Arby's Bronco Berry Sauce (Copycat)

*A sweet-and-spicy jelly sauce copycat of Arby's dipping sauce — despite the name, there's no actual berry in it.*

**Servings:** Makes 1 cup | **Prep Time:** ~10 min | **Cook Time:** ~10 min

## About This Recipe

Arby's serves this alongside its battered jalapeño-and-cheese bites in small single-serving cups — never quite enough to use elsewhere. This copycat version makes a full cup, useful well beyond dipping fried finger foods: try it as a sweet-spicy substitute for mint jelly with lamb chops.

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup water
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup corn syrup
- 3 tablespoons pectin powder
- 2 teaspoons cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon white vinegar
- 1/4 teaspoon red food coloring
- 1/8 teaspoon onion powder
- Pinch cayenne pepper
- Pinch garlic powder
- Pinch paprika
- 1/4 cup minced red bell pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon minced canned jalapeño peppers

## Directions

1. Combine all ingredients except the bell pepper and jalapeño in a small saucepan and whisk well.
2. Set the saucepan over medium-high heat, uncovered. Add the bell pepper and jalapeño and bring to a full boil, stirring often.
3. Reduce the heat and simmer for 5 to 7 minutes, or until thickened. Remove from heat and let sit for about 10 minutes, then stir and cover.
4. Use at room temperature, or cover and chill until needed.

## Notes & Tips

- Despite the name, there's no fruit in this sauce at all — the color and sweetness come from the sugar and red food coloring.
