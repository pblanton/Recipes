# Barbacoa

*Garlicky, tender shredded beef, braised low and slow in a spicy, smoky sauce.*

**Servings:** 8–10 | **Prep Time:** 40 min | **Cook Time:** 8 hours | **Total Time:** 8 hours 40 min

## About This Recipe

Slow-cooker shredded beef built on a blended chipotle-and-citrus sauce, finished by reducing the cooking liquid back down into a proper sauce and giving the meat a quick griddle sear. Great in burritos, soft tacos, or over cilantro-lime rice.

![Barbacoa](./Barbacoa.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 6 to 8 pounds boneless chuck roast, trimmed of excess fat and cut into fist-sized chunks
- Chipotles in adobo sauce, to taste
- 1 large onion, peeled and roughly chopped (purple onion preferred)
- 10 cloves garlic, peeled and roughly chopped
- 1 cup beef or chicken broth, or a golden lager beer (avoid a hoppy, strong IPA)
- 2/3 cup apple cider vinegar, preferably raw
- 1/4 cup lime juice, preferably fresh
- 2 tablespoons cumin
- 2 tablespoons oregano, preferably Mexican oregano (most oregano sold in US grocery stores is actually Mexican sage, which works fine here)
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon ground black pepper

## Directions

1. Add the beef to the slow-cooker crock.
2. In a blender, combine the chipotles, onion, garlic, broth or beer, vinegar, lime juice, cumin, oregano, cloves, salt, and black pepper. Blend on high until smooth.
3. Pour the sauce over the beef and cover. Ideally, let it marinate in the refrigerator overnight. In the morning, set the slow cooker to LOW and cook for 8 hours, or until the beef is very tender.
4. Drain the liquid and reduce it in a skillet over low heat until it thickens into a sauce.
5. Pull the drained beef into taco- or burrito-sized chunks and finish it on a hot griddle until it's browned to your liking.

## Notes & Tips

- If marinating overnight isn't practical, you can go straight to the slow cooker — the flavor will still be good, just slightly less deep.
- The griddle-searing step at the end adds a nice char and isn't strictly necessary if you're short on time.
