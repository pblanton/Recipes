# Gluten-Free Flour Tortillas

*Gluten-free option. A pliable flour tortilla built on a Cup4Cup and masa harina blend, with the masa adding flavor and helping the dough absorb water.*

**Servings:** About 12 taco-size tortillas | **Prep Time:** ~10 min | **Cook Time:** A few minutes per tortilla | **Total Time:** ~20 min

## About This Recipe

Straight Cup4Cup would work, but blending in masa harina adds flavor and texture and helps the dough absorb the water more readily. Very scalable — the ratio holds for larger batches.

<!-- Add photo: ./Gluten-Free-Flour-Tortillas.jpg -->

## Ingredients

- 1 cup Cup4Cup gluten-free flour
- 1 cup Bob's Red Mill gluten-free masa harina
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon xanthan gum (optional, but helps with dough body)
- ~2 tablespoons avocado oil
- 1 cup hot water

## Directions

1. Combine the flour, masa harina, salt, and xanthan gum (if using).
2. Add the oil and hot water, then knead with your hands until the dough is no longer sticky — texture should be like play dough.
3. Press or roll into 12 taco-size rounds.
4. Cook the tortillas in a cast iron skillet with a bit more avocado oil.

## Notes & Tips

- Cup4Cup is the most expensive gluten-free flour on the market; cutting it 1:1 with masa harina helps offset the cost while still holding together well.
- Avocado oil is the right call here as-is — it's already the binder's preferred oil, and its high smoke point suits the skillet cooking step.
- For the best chilaquiles chips: chop leftover or extra tortillas and pan-toast them in a little oil.
