# Whole Roasted Pumpkin with Green Beans and Red Curry Rice

*A whole pumpkin used as both cooking vessel and ingredient, filled with red curry rice that turns risotto-like as it bakes.*

**Servings:** 4 to 6 | **Prep Time (Active):** 10 min | **Total Time:** 2 hours

## About This Recipe

Long Island cheese pumpkins have deep orange, sweet, nutty flesh that roasts smooth and creamy — ideal for using as an edible vessel here. The rice cooks inside the pumpkin with coconut milk and red curry paste until it takes on a risotto-like texture, with tender scoops of pumpkin flesh mixed through. Bring the whole pumpkin to the table and spoon the rice and pumpkin into bowls; it also makes a good side dish for roast fish or lamb.

![Whole Roasted Pumpkin with Green Beans and Red Curry Rice](./Whole-Roasted-Pumpkin-with-Green-Beans-and-Red-Curry-Rice.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 1 (3-pound) Long Island cheese pumpkin, scrubbed
- 1 cup uncooked jasmine rice
- 1 (15 oz) can coconut milk
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 (3 to 4 oz) can red curry paste
- 2 cups green beans, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce, or more to taste
- Kosher salt, to taste
- Fresh cilantro leaves, fresh mint leaves, and fried shallots, for serving

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Using a small sharp knife, cut a 4-inch circle around the pumpkin's stem to make a lid. Trim any stringy membrane from the underside of the lid and set aside. Scrape out the seeds and stringy membrane from inside the pumpkin.
2. Set the pumpkin on a parchment- or foil-lined rimmed baking sheet. Add the rice, coconut milk, water, and curry paste to the pumpkin and stir to dissolve the curry paste. Replace the lid.
3. Bake until the filling is bubbling, about 1 hour. Carefully remove the lid and stir in the green beans, fish sauce, and salt to taste. Replace the lid and continue baking at 350°F until the pumpkin flesh and green beans are fork-tender and the rice has a risotto-like texture, 30 to 45 minutes more.
4. Let stand 15 minutes, then taste and adjust the seasoning with salt or fish sauce as needed.
5. Scoop the rice and tender pumpkin into bowls and top with cilantro, mint, and fried shallots.

## Notes & Tips

- Line the baking sheet well — the pumpkin can release juices during baking.
- Make-ahead: the pumpkin can be cut open and cleaned out up to a day ahead and stored in the refrigerator until ready to fill and bake.
