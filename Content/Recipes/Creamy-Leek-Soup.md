# Creamy Leek Soup

*A simple, brothy leek and potato soup finished with a splash of white wine and milk.*

**Servings:** Not specified in original | **Prep Time:** Not specified | **Cook Time:** ~10 min active | **Total Time:** Not specified

## About This Recipe

A straightforward, five-minutes-of-effort soup: potatoes and aromatics get a quick sauté, leeks and wine simmer in, and everything gets blended smooth with milk stirred back in at the end. Good candidate for doubling and freezing.

![Creamy Leek Soup](./Creamy-Leek-Soup.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1 cup diced potato
- 1/2 cup thinly sliced green onions (scallions)
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon dried thyme or oregano leaves
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 1/2 pounds white leeks, sliced (about 9 cups)
- 1 cup white wine or water
- 1 1/2 cups low-fat milk

## Directions

1. Melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium-high heat.
2. Add the potato, scallions, garlic, salt, thyme, and pepper. Cook, stirring often, until the onions just begin to brown, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the leeks and the wine (or water); bring to a boil and cook for 1 minute.
4. Using a slotted spoon, remove about 1 cup of the vegetables and set aside.
5. Working in batches, purée the remaining soup in a blender or food processor until smooth, transferring each batch to a bowl.
6. Return the puréed soup to the saucepan, stir in the milk and the reserved vegetables, and simmer just until heated through, about 5 minutes.
7. Garnish with parsley, if desired.

## Notes & Tips

- Cook a double batch and freeze half for another dinner.
- The original instructions mention adding "broth" along with the leeks and wine, but no broth appears in the ingredient list — likely an error carried over from the source site. As written above, the leeks go in with just the wine (or water).
