# Dr Pepper Texas Sheet Cake

*A thin, moist chocolate sheet cake made with Dr Pepper, topped with warm fudge icing and toasted pecans.*

**Servings:** 16 | **Prep Time (Active):** 20 min | **Total Time:** 2 hours 5 min

## About This Recipe

A classic Texas sheet cake — baked thin on a rimmed sheet pan, then topped with warm fudge icing while both cake and icing are still warm so the icing soaks in slightly at the edges. Dr Pepper stands in for the usual cola or water in both the cake and the icing.

![Dr Pepper Texas Sheet Cake](./Dr-Pepper-Cake.jpg)

## Ingredients

**Sheet Cake**
- 1 1/2 cups Dr Pepper (One 12oz. can. Uses the whole can.)
- 1 cup butter
- 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 large eggs, lightly beaten
- 1/2 cup whole buttermilk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

**Rich Fudge Icing**
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa
- 1/4 cup Dr Pepper (1/3 of a 12oz. can. Drink the rest. It's delicious)
- 2 tablespoons whole milk
- 4 cups powdered sugar, sifted
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

**To Finish**
- 1 1/2 cups coarsely chopped, toasted pecans

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Coat a 17 1/2 x 12 1/2-inch rimmed baking sheet with cooking spray.
2. Bring the Dr Pepper, butter, and cocoa to a boil in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat, stirring often. Remove from heat.
3. Whisk together the flour, sugar, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt in a large bowl. Add the Dr Pepper mixture and whisk until blended. Whisk in the eggs, buttermilk, and vanilla.
4. Pour the batter into the prepared baking sheet. Bake until a wooden pick inserted in the center comes out clean, 16 to 20 minutes.
5. While the cake bakes, make the icing: cook the butter and cocoa in a medium saucepan over medium-low heat, whisking often, until the butter melts and the mixture is smooth, about 1 minute.
6. Reduce the heat to low and whisk in the Dr Pepper and milk until blended. Gradually whisk in the powdered sugar until smooth, then whisk in the vanilla.
7. Pour the warm icing over the warm cake and gently spread into an even layer. Sprinkle with the pecans.
8. Cool completely in the pan on a wire rack, about 1 hour.

## Notes & Tips

- Pouring the icing over the cake while both are still warm is what gives this its classic thin, glossy fudge topping — don't let the cake cool fully first.
