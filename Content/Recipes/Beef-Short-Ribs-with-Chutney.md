# Beef Short Ribs with Chutney

*Boneless short ribs braised with tomatoes, fig preserves, and rosemary, with a smoky base from beef bacon.*

**Servings:** 4 | **Prep Time:** 30 min | **Cook Time:** 35 min (pressure cooker) plus natural release, or 6–8 hours (slow cooker)

## About This Recipe

A fig-and-rosemary braise for boneless short ribs, with the option to build it in either a pressure cooker or a slow cooker. The tomatoes cook down into the base of the sauce, and the preserves add a sweet counterpoint to the richness of the beef.

## Ingredients

- 1 teaspoon olive oil
- 3 strips beef bacon, chopped
- 1 pound boneless beef short ribs
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper
- 1 pound grape tomatoes
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 cups water
- 1 cup Marsala wine or beef broth
- 1/4 cup fig preserves
- 3 tablespoons minced fresh rosemary, or 1 tablespoon dried rosemary, crushed

## Directions

**Pressure Cooker Method**

1. Select the sauté setting on a 6-quart electric pressure cooker, adjusted to medium heat, and add the oil. Once hot, cook the beef bacon, stirring, until crisp. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels.
2. Sprinkle the ribs with salt and pepper and brown on all sides in the drippings. Remove from the pot.
3. Add the tomatoes, onion, and garlic to the drippings; cook, stirring frequently and mashing the tomatoes lightly, until crisp-tender, 3–5 minutes.
4. Stir in the water, Marsala, preserves, and rosemary, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot. Cook 1 minute.
5. Return the ribs and beef bacon to the pot. Lock the lid, close the pressure-release valve, and cook on high pressure for 35 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally.
6. Remove the ribs, shred with two forks, and serve with the reserved cooking juices.

**Slow Cooker Method**

1. In a large skillet, heat the oil over medium heat and cook the beef bacon until crisp. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels.
2. Sprinkle the ribs with salt and pepper and brown on all sides in the drippings, then transfer to a 4-quart slow cooker.
3. In the same skillet, add the tomatoes, onion, and garlic to the drippings; cook, stirring frequently and mashing the tomatoes lightly, until crisp-tender, 3–5 minutes.
4. Add the tomato mixture to the slow cooker along with the water, Marsala, preserves, and rosemary.
5. Cook, covered, on low for 6–8 hours, or until the ribs are tender.
6. Remove the ribs, shred with two forks, and serve with the reserved cooking juices.

## Notes & Tips

- Beef bacon stands in for pork bacon here to keep the recipe clean — look for it at halal or kosher markets, or some larger grocery chains carry it now. It gives the same smoky rendered-fat base the original recipe is built on.
