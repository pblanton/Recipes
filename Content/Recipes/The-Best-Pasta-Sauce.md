# Marcella Hazan's Tomato-Butter Pasta Sauce

*A famously simple three-ingredient tomato sauce, popularized by Marcella Hazan.*

**Servings:** Enough for about 1 pound of pasta | **Prep Time:** 5 min | **Cook Time:** 45 min

## About This Recipe

Marcella Hazan's tomato sauce skips the usual chopping and sautéing entirely — whole tomatoes, a halved onion, and a stick's worth of butter simmer together untouched, and the onion is discarded at the end. It looks almost too plain to work, but the long, slow simmer is doing all the work.

![Marcella Hazan's Tomato-Butter Pasta Sauce](./The-Best-Pasta-Sauce.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 1 can (28 oz) whole, peeled tomatoes
- 1 onion, peeled and halved
- 5 tablespoons butter
- Salt, to taste

## Directions

1. Combine the tomatoes, onion halves, and butter in a single pot.
2. Simmer over medium heat, uncovered, for 45 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Remove and discard the onion halves. Season with salt and pour over your favorite pasta.

## Notes & Tips

- The onion and butter sit in the pot largely intact rather than being chopped or sautéed first — that's not a mistake, it's the whole method.

## Source

A three-ingredient tomato sauce from Marcella Hazan's *Essentials of Classic Italian Cooking*, as covered by Delish and Epicurious.
